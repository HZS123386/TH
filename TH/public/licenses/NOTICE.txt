ThaiRasiWheel.tsx adapts the geometry, sign orientation and symbol positions
from scripts/render-readme-chart.cjs at the pinned commit. Adaptations: React
SVG rendering, responsive square viewBox, WANJAI colors, live account data,
divisional-chart selector, text fitting and accessibility labels.

Location names, WGS84 coordinates and timezone identifiers are adapted by the
upstream project from GeoNames: https://www.geonames.org/

Upstream data source and modifications:

WANJAI uses the packaged records, filters the search UI and permits user-supplied
coordinates; a provincial-seat point does not identify a specific hospital.
The landing page's birth-province directory adds 34 Chinese provincial-level
options and 77 Thai provincial-level options in backend/content/birth-regions.json.
Names and search aliases are adapted from the GeoNames country and alternate-name
extracts retrieved on 2026-11-08. Chinese/Thai display translations were completed
editorially. Coordinates represent provincial capitals/seats; the packaged Thai
seat coordinates are retained. These adapted data remain under CC BY 4.0.
scripts/build-birth-locations.py additionally indexes the complete CN and TH
country extracts and localized alternate names for offline city recognition.
Historical/abandoned settlements are excluded; packaged starter coordinates
are retained for existing IDs. The generated data/birth-locations.sqlite3
contains adapted GeoNames data under the same CC BY 4.0 terms.

The RoxyAPI/ai-spiritual-companion repository was evaluated, not copied or
installed. Its Supabase, pgvector, Next.js and Roxy MCP stack is not included.
WANJAI's Thai prompts and tool routing are authored in this project.

Validation fixtures under scripts/fixtures/thai are numerical facts attributed
to the pinned upstream MIT project, USNO and IANA. Four USNO JSON responses
under reports/thai-evidence were retrieved independently on 2026-11-05 using
synthetic public dates and coordinates; they contain no user birth records.

The twelve illustrations under images/zodiac/illustrated were generated with the
built-in image_gen tool on 2026-11-09 for ASTRO AI. Source PNGs and the exact
generation prompts are retained under assets/zodiac/illustrated. Existing zodiac
artwork outside that directory remains unchanged.
